Waveshare sent me its **HomeRack 8U**, a compact **10-inch server rack** made from aluminum alloy.

What makes this rack interesting is its premium design and the ability to mount not only networking equipment, but also **Raspberry Pis, single-board computers, storage, and even a touchscreen display**.

For my setup, I turned it into a compact home lab with a **Home Assistant monitoring dashboard**.

## Premium Aluminum Design

The HomeRack arrives completely disassembled, with all the major parts required to build the rack.

Inside the package you get:

-   Aluminum side panels
    
-   Top and bottom plates
    
-   Handles and feet
    
-   10-inch rack shelf
    
-   SBC mounting shelf
    
-   Blank rack plates
    
-   Raspberry Pi adapter boards
    
-   Screws, spacers, and assembly tools
    

The aluminum parts are precisely cut and fit together nicely, giving the finished rack a **premium metal appearance**.

## Mounting Raspberry Pi and SBCs

The included SBC shelf supports several popular development boards.

It can be used with boards such as:

-   Raspberry Pi
    
-   Radxa Rock 5B
    
-   Jetson Orin
    
-   Other compatible SBCs
    

There are also mounting points for **SSDs and hard drives**.

For my rack, I installed a Raspberry Pi using the supplied adapter board and spacers.

I also mounted my **Radxa Dragon Q8B** alongside it.

This keeps both boards neatly organized instead of leaving them loose on a desk.

## Adding a 7-Inch Touchscreen

One of my favorite additions is the optional **7-inch capacitive touchscreen**.

The display mounts directly onto the front of the rack, while a Raspberry Pi sits neatly behind it.

The Raspberry Pi powers and controls the screen, so I only need a power connection going to the Pi.

For my setup, the screen displays a **Home Assistant dashboard** showing performance information from the devices running inside my rack and around my home.

## Price and Final Thoughts

The main downside is the price.

I found the rack costing roughly **€230 on Amazon** and around **€190 on AliExpress including shipping**, depending on the seller.

There are definitely cheaper DIY options, But with the HomeRack, you are paying for:

-   Premium aluminum construction
    
-   Clean 10-inch rack design
    
-   SBC mounting support
    
-   Expandability
    
-   Optional touchscreen integration
    
-   A much cleaner overall home lab setup
    

If your priority is building the cheapest possible home lab, there are better alternatives.

But if you want a **premium-looking 10-inch server rack** for Raspberry Pis, networking equipment, Home Assistant, and other home lab hardware, the **Waveshare HomeRack 8U** is an interesting option.
